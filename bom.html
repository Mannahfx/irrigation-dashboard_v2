Power Architecture Note:
Because the ESP32 and the sensors require 5V / 3.3V but the heavy actuators (Valve, Pump, Fan) require 12V, the best wiring approach is:
1. Connect the 12V Power Adapter directly to the Relays (to power the Valve/Pump/Fan).
2. Connect the 12V Power Adapter to the LM2596 Buck Converter.
3. Tune the Buck Converter output to exactly 5V and feed that into the VIN (or 5V) pin of the ESP32 to power the brain safely.
